# Resource adoption

**Text uses Public Pixel; symbols and emoji use Unscii and Public Pixel; kana uses Misaki. The palette is DB8, the CRT renderer is CRT-Lottes, and sound follows AY-3-8910 specifications and measurements.**

This document records what is adopted, why, and from which version. [Font coverage](glyphs.md) defines the repertoire, bitmap priority, conversions, colors and reproduction. [README](../README.md#license) lists licenses; original notices are retained in `data/fonts/`.

| [Misaki Gothic](https://littlelimit.net/misaki.htm) — Num Kadoma / 門真なむ                                                         | An 8×8 Japanese typeface made for PC-E500 pocket computers, with artwork mostly within 7×7 pixels to leave spacing. Supplies hiragana and katakana.  | **2021-05-05 BDF release**, `misaki_gothic.bdf`.                       |

**Every bundled preset uses source-font artwork.** There are no project-original presets or bitmaps assembled from multiple fonts. The repertoire follows fixed sources and Unicode categories, without selecting countries, languages or individual characters by preference. Exact sets and counts are in [letters and kana](glyphs.md#established-repertoire) and [symbols and emoji](glyphs.md#symbols-and-emoji); overlaps follow [bitmap selection](glyphs.md#exact-bitmap-selection).

Artwork is not scaled, blurred or cropped. Cell-position restoration, whole-bitmap translation and size are documented under [conversion and cost](glyphs.md#conversion-and-cost). [Requests outside the scope](glyphs.md#requests-outside-the-scope) explains missing source artwork. Game authors can define their own patterns through `pcg`.

Twemoji's original [CC BY 4.0 graphics license](https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji/blob/b6b55fef1e8636b540a6d016a4729ca8cdf2e60b/LICENSE-GRAPHICS) is retained in `data/fonts/twemoji-color-LICENSE.txt`. [Common color rules and results](glyphs.md#emoji-colors) are recorded in one place, without per-character overrides. Contrast thresholds and other parameters are Playload design choices, not Unicode color rules or a claim of WCAG compliance.

[Retro output design](retro-output.md) documents CRT adjustments, the CPU fallback when WebGL is unavailable, and browser audio processing. These do not reproduce measurements of a particular analog board or speaker. CRT and sound default to on; browser audio permission requires user interaction.

## Brand images

[Press Start 2P](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Press+Start+2P) supplies the logo's letter shapes. The finished `logo.svg` and `favicon.svg` are static images, separate from the game bank. The original TTF, fixed revision and SIL OFL 1.1 notice are retained in `data/fonts/`.

## Provenance and verification

Original sources, conversion records, input/output SHA-256 hashes and per-glyph sources are retained. Ordinary font regeneration is offline. [Font coverage](glyphs.md#browsing-and-reproduction) documents source-bitmap comparisons and actual worker-rendering checks. Unicode supplies keys, names and classifications, not emoji artwork.

Independent comparisons use **emu2149 v1.42** for sound and the **frozen original CRT-Lottes** for display. emu2149 is a test reference, not part of the engine. [Retro validation](retro-validation.md) records conditions, differences and unverified areas. Source attribution does not imply endorsement by the authors or expert approval.
